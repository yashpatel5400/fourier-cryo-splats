# Matched Fourier Gaussian baselines for the locked target family

Development comparison specified before its baseline outcomes. Evaluate every
locked pilot target on exactly the 128 inference particles, radius-12 samples,
prescribed noise scale, unit-L2 pilot, and unit-L2 64-cell reference used by the
continuous fit. Geometry seed is 609315. The first continuous 10049 region-1
fit is already complete; its targets were locked earlier and are not revised.
This is an extension of the completed Fourier VI baseline to the new estimands,
not another frozen confirmation and not experimental-density calibration.

Use the existing Hermitian trilinear operator on an odd 33-cubed Fourier grid.
The Gaussian prior mean is the orthogonal projection of the continuous pilot
onto that finite Fourier basis. Prior coordinate SD is s/sqrt(33^3) with all
three previously used deviation-norm scales s=0.5, 1, 2. Retain every scale;
do not select one using reference performance. This prior does not assert a
hard density-radius bound. Normalizing widths by 2 times the continuous target
norm is only a common display scale, not an equivalence of prior assumptions.

Compare the exact full Gaussian posterior and the exact diagonal-Gaussian VI
optimum for the same likelihood, prior mean and prior scale. As in the existing
baseline, this implements Ullrich et al.'s equation-14 objective independently,
with CTFs and the project's real Fourier coordinates; it is not execution of
the original authors' full pipeline. Use alpha=0.05/12 per target for each fixed
prior scale. Do not claim a family guarantee across selected prior scales.

Report the analytic prior-predictive variance identity and coverage as a model
check. Separately report fixed-reference expected centers, widths, coverage and
correct-sign probability for the model-matched reference, the continuous
reference at nominal poses, and coherent/random-boundary perturbations at
0/1/2 degrees with 0.5 A shifts. Reuse the pose audit's exact random-boundary
seed 610281 + accession for every angle, including the shift-only case. The
coherent-x case has only a rotation component and duplicates nominal poses
when its rotation budget is zero; it is retained, not counted as new evidence.

These fixed-pose baselines do not claim robustness to unknown poses or uniform
frequentist coverage over a density ball. Reference failures measure a different
property from prior-predictive coverage. Retain interpolation error, all prior
scales, all targets and every numerical failure. No additional Monte Carlo
replicates are needed for the analytic Gaussian probabilities.
