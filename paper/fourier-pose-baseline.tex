\subsection{A local Gaussian pose-marginalization baseline}
The preceding comparison holds poses fixed. A separately declared extension
includes Gaussian pose errors through the derivative of the same trilinear
pilot signal. This relates to the need for coupled density/alignment analysis
identified by \citet{rangan2024}, but is not a reproduction of their volumetric
Hessian method. In the approximate model
$y=Ax+Ju+\epsilon$, let $x\sim N(m,\tau^2I)$, $u\sim N(0,I)$ and
$\epsilon\sim N(0,I)$ independently. Each particle's five columns of $J$
include its supplied pose standard deviations. With $K=I+JJ^\top$,
\[
 Q=A^\top K^{-1}A+\tau^{-2}I,\qquad
 v=Q^{-1}\ell,\quad w=K^{-1}Av.
\]
Ordinary Gaussian conditioning gives posterior feature variance $\ell^\top v$
and the check
\[
 \ell^\top v=\|w\|^2+\|J^\top w\|^2
                 +\tau^2\|A^\top w-\ell\|^2.
\]
Independent small particle blocks apply $K^{-1/2}$ without a dense global
covariance. Tests compare the resulting posterior with a separately assembled
joint density/pose precision and check the Jacobian against nonlinear finite
differences. This is standard Gaussian algebra, not an additional statistical
contribution.

The bounded comparison retains all twelve features, both previously inspected
coordinate prior SDs $0.1$ and $1$, and the fixed-pose controls. Local pose SDs
are one degree per rotation coordinate and $0.5\,\AA$ per detector shift.
Their norms are Gaussian and unbounded; they are not the audit's hard joint
unit ball. The target frame remains fixed by the prior. All 48 fits complete,
with exactly reproduced fixed-pose widths and maximum variance-identity
relative discrepancy $2.93\times10^{-13}$. Total setup and fitting take
4.6--8.5 seconds per stack, with cumulative process peak memory at most
1.27 GB. The 384 analytic reference checks are implementation and sensitivity
calculations, not independent experimental replicates.

Table~\ref{tab:fourier-pose} retains both favorable and unfavorable results.
Local pose marginalization increases widths by $0.0066$--$0.2875\%$.
Some continuous-reference cases remain below nominal coverage, despite exact
matched-prior calibration. Small width changes apply only to this pilot
linearization. In the prescribed one-degree coherent/random configurations,
its forward discrepancy from the nonlinear trilinear pilot is
$0.71$--$2.41\%$ of signal norm; at two degrees it is $2.00$--$6.71\%$.
The corresponding one-degree error is $14.1$--$27.3\%$ of the pose-induced
signal change. Density/pose products are also omitted. These observations do
not establish that full nonlinear pose uncertainty is negligible, or that
the Gaussian pose prior is calibrated for the experimental particles.
