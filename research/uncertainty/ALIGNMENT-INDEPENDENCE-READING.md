# Alignment, cross-validation and independence: targeted source checks

1 October 2026 UTC. These readings constrain interpretation of the new phase-only control. They do not establish novelty or supply experimental calibration.

**Jensen (2001), Alignment error envelopes for single particle analysis.** [Primary author abstract indexed by PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11472086/), DOI [10.1006/jsbi.2001.4334](https://doi.org/10.1006/jsbi.2001.4334). Abstract only: incoherent averaging from misalignment is modeled through error envelopes, including location, orientation, defocus, magnification and beam tilt. This is direct historical context for signal attenuation from pose errors. No full-text theorem or quantitative envelope is claimed to have been checked.

**Shaikh, Hegerl and Frank (2003), An approach to examining model dependence in EM reconstructions using cross-validation.** [Primary author abstract indexed by PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12713958/), DOI [10.1016/S1047-8477(03)00029-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1047-8477(03)00029-7). Abstract only: the work separates fitting and validation observations in projection matching and examines reference dependence. It reports different behavior on real and noise-only inputs. It does not, in the inspected abstract, promise confidence-interval coverage of unknown density. Cross-validation against alignment bias therefore cannot be presented as a new idea here.

**Van et al. (2025), probabilistic ab initio reconstruction in SIMPLE.** [Primary full text](https://journals.iucr.org/d/issues/2025/08/00/ic5124/), DOI [10.1107/S2059798325005686](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2059798325005686). Read abstract, introduction, method opening and discussion; no full proof/code replication. The method updates a shared orientation-assignment probability table during particle assignments, deliberately coupling decisions across particles. Its class-balanced sampling and adaptive regularization further distinguish the complete pipeline from independently fitting particles against a fixed template. The discussion retains user choices of state count and heterogeneity mode. Our independent-particle local-template simulation is therefore a controlled special case, not a proxy for every probabilistic reconstruction pipeline.

The existing bias/variance survey and the moments reading note cover related material. The phase-control theorem is our elementary derivation; these papers are cited for historical scope, not as sources of its exact coverage formula.
