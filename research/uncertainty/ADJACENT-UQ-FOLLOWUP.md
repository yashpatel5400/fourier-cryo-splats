# Targeted follow-up: anisotropy and Gaussian-splat uncertainty

1 October 2026 UTC. Four additions bring the candidate ledger to 130. Reading scope and retrieved-file hashes are in `adjacent-uq-followup-sources.json`; none of these implementations was run.

- [Yadgar and Lederman, 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.15.738625): primary bioRxiv API abstract only; JATS access returned HTTP 429. The authors connect anisotropic Fourier uncertainty to atomic-model fitting and describe a weighted-volume interpretation under stated modeling choices. The full derivation remains unchecked. This further separates atomic fitting from confidence intervals for density features.
- [Jia et al., arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05522v1): read the NIW construction, approximation boundary and limitations. Updates are conjugate given renderer-derived surrogate summaries; the paper does not claim exact Bayesian inference through its entire renderer. Its native predictive intervals and camera-selection tests concern photographic scenes. Transplanting the conjugate algebra would not establish a cryo-EM likelihood or density-coverage guarantee.
- [Galappaththige et al., arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22786v1): read methods and the main uncertainty evaluation. A fixed renderer attributes observed photometric residuals to primitive channels through regularized least squares. Correlation and sparsification metrics test error ranking. This is useful prior art for Gaussian uncertainty channels, with a different statistical target from a density interval.
- [Chu et al., arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10307v1): read methods, validity argument and relevant proofs/limits. A discrete inner quantile scores each view's covered pixel fraction; an outer split-conformal quantile uses exchangeable labeled views. Symmetric label-free batch processing preserves the rank argument. The guarantee is marginal over views, and ordered paths can violate its assumption. It does not turn voxels or correlated particles into independent calibration molecules.

These readings reinforce the need to specify the calibration unit and predictive target. They do not motivate retroactively changing the frozen local-pose study. The new atomic paper is direct cryo-EM context; the three graphics papers constrain claims about novelty of Gaussian uncertainty representations.
