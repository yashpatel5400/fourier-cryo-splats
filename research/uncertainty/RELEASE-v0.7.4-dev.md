# Conditional noise and viewing uncertainty — development checkpoint

The 30-page ICML-format draft adds a conditional-noise envelope, explicit
amplitude/noise independence, classical CVaR and variance baselines, and
preferred-view controls. All three full Claude Fable 5.1 reviews remain
rejections. The new authentic focused audit checks the conditional argument,
identifies missing assumptions/comparisons, and is followed by an evidence-linked
response. It is not a fourth full review or an acceptance verdict.

This increment retains all 360 initial, 480 comparator and 1,944 preferred-view
projections, both correctly specified null maps, and 648 cells of actual
64-group outcomes. The preferred-view generator uses 1,179,648 independent
rotations across 18 stack/law cases, evaluating all three amplitudes and spatial
axes. Methods/maps/amplitudes share draws. Calibration is fixed, so those groups
do not establish unconditional error control over calibration repetitions.

The paired bound improves over the implemented classical comparators at the
saved budget, but its variance upper bounds are dominated by concentration
slack. Sensitivity depends strongly on amplitude, larger viewing ratios remain
weak, and the 10076 zero scores remain unresolved. There is no new three-stack
positive result, experimental nuisance calibration or practical non-oracle test.

Nineteen targeted tests pass. Independent replays check all new event counts
and thresholds, repeat-group vectors, first direct scores, frequency geometry
and transfer provenance. A separately seeded 1% replay checks 167,936 moment
cubics using different Fourier-sum and polynomial implementations. These are
numerical checks, not a validated arithmetic certificate or novelty assessment.
