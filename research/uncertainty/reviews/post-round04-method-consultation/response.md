## Decisions and qualifications

We accept the priority ordering: close the moment branch; perform a bounded
population materiality screen before building a new uncertainty method;
finish one numerical integration repair and end that numerical branch. A
favorable arithmetic screen would not establish experimental calibration,
novelty, or a publishable contribution. No further full review is requested
without a substantive scientific result.

The [population screen protocol](../../POPULATION-MATERIALITY-PROTOCOL.md)
fixes the target, law proxies, thresholds and all sensitivity analyses before
new outcomes. The [algebra audit](../../POPULATION-SCREEN-ALGEBRA.md) separates
an exact known-pose identity from weak-signal and amplitude-tangent surrogates.
In particular:

- E2's odds equation is implicit: its discriminability depends on the fitted
  population. We compute its exact Gaussian root as well as the approximation.
- Recorded source halves and published filtering groups are metadata groups,
  not biological state labels. All three downloaded `.cs` files have constant
  class 0 and class posterior 1. None supplies the requested class-specific
  viewing distributions. Histogram reweighting is a specified law proxy, not
  a recovered latent law.
- Real amplitude uses the square of the real inner product, not the squared
  modulus of a complex inner product. Removing that tangent gives local mean
  separation, not efficient population information with arbitrary amplitudes.
  The known-amplitude unbiased-score variance formula does not become an
  amplitude-robust theorem by replacing its denominator.
- The nominal SE .03 criterion at n=10,000 and population .75 corresponds to
  harmonic term 8.8125, not 9. We use the exact arithmetic threshold. Even a
  pass is only an optimistic feasibility screen.
- The alpha–view figures .0262 and .0074 are fractions of recorded alpha
  variance explained by bins. They are not 2.6% and .74% amplitude ranges;
  they cannot calibrate or justify narrowing the amplitude nuisance class.
- A failed materiality screen would not establish general adequacy of pooled
  likelihood. A failed harmonic screen would not establish that occupancy is
  globally unanswerable. Each conclusion is limited to this region, band,
  simulator and specified law proxies.

D1 is accepted in its one-sided, descriptive sense: a frozen template-bank

Its observed separation can be compared with the moments. Neither its D value
nor an inferred particle count is a KL divergence, ceiling or guaranteed
