## Verified identities

The following check out by direct derivation from the source and are not repeated as findings.

- **Density majorant.** With `eta = E * sum(transfer**2)`, Cauchy–Schwarz gives `E (sum |t_j| b_j)^2 <= eta ||w||^2`, so `G_Q + eta I - G` is PSD and `G_Q` itself is PSD because the quadrature weights are positive. `H(w)^2 = ||ell - A0*w||^2 + w^T (G_Q + eta I - G) w` is therefore a squared affine Hilbert norm, and the derivative `(G_Q w + eta w - a)/H` in `CubicDesignObjective.evaluate` is right. The Gram-action pad `sqrt(2) E ||transfer||_F sum|c|` follows from expanding `Re(field) e^{-2 pi i k_j x}` into exponentials of frequencies `k_l ± k_j`, each with error at most `E` per real or imaginary part.
- **Pose support.** For unit `v_j` and `u_j = F(w)* v_j / ||F(w)* v_j||`, the gradient of `v_j^T F(w) u_j` is a support of the operator norm since `F` is linear in `w`. `mass_terms` returns half the gradient of `E6 ||M b||^2`, so `(field*field_gradient + pad_gradient)/pose_norm` equals `(s/D) g_F + (p/D)^T sqrt(E6) M grad b`, and `(s/D)^2 + ||p/D||^2 = 1` exactly. The amplitude support uses Euler homogeneity of `b_j` and nonnegativity of `M` and `p`. The mixing weights sum to one, and `g_F^T w = lse - smoothing * entropy <= lse`, matching the "Euler not asserted" remark.
- **Continuous dual.** With `v = f(ell - A0*w)`, the expansion `<v, ell - A0*w'> = f(||ell||^2 - a^T w) - f(a - G w)^T w'` has the signs used in `audit`; scaling by `c = min(1, z/d)` keeps `c v` in the ball, keeps `c g_J` a support, and makes `||A0(cv) - c g_J|| <= z`. Omitting `eta w` from `a - G_Q w` is correct because the dual pairs with the true operator, and the triangle inequality with `e_G` covers `(G_Q - G) w`. `max(0, ...)` is valid since the objective is nonnegative.
- **Order matching.** Both the optimizer and `audit` use the same `objective.op` at pose order 64 for the field, its degree-six pad, and the fresh power certificate on `spatial_gram`, and the same order-80 `gram` for `H` and the Gram-action pad. The protocol's warning about mixing orders is respected.
- **Sobolev penalty.** `derivative_gram_blocks` reproduces `ball_sobolev_norms` for `c = t(w_r + i w_i)`: the `|f|^2` term gives `w_r^T D w_r + w_i^T D w_i`, the `Re f^2` term gives `(-1)^m (w_r^T A w_r - w_i^T A w_i)`, and the `w_r`/`w_i` cross terms vanish by symmetry of the kernels. Multipliers `partial_bell(path)[order, 1:]/order!` match `higher_order_particle_remainders` at degree 3, the subgradient `multipliers * G w / norm` is exact away from zero and zero at zero, and the diagonal pads only enlarge the value.
- **Preconditioning.** `gram_spectral_power` applies `(ridge I + P)^{power}` blockwise; the factor columns have norm `sqrt(lam)`, and the `/lam` restores the correct projector, so half and negative-half powers invert. The chain rule for the gradient is right because the transform is symmetric. Validity of the audit does not depend on the preconditioner.
- **Probability bookkeeping.** `gaussian_power_upper` uses `c = sqrt(2) erfinv(delta^(1/m))`, the union of all iterations is on the same probe event, and `min` over steps is valid. Probes with seed 650001 are drawn after weights are fixed, the optimization seed only sets `v0` for ARPACK, and `alpha_noise + delta = alpha`.
- **Zero-weight optimum.** `J >= 0`, `H >= ||ell - A0*w|| >= ||ell|| - a^T w/||ell||`, so the objective is at least `B||ell|| + (z - B||a||/||ell||)||w||`. The analytic test condition is sufficient.

## Findings

**W1. Medium. Floating-point guards do not cover the NUFFT tolerance.**
Location: `CubicDesignObjective.density` guard `256*eps*(...)`; `QuadratureObservationGram.matvec` with `eps=1e-12`; `audit` defect using `gw`; `gaussian_power_upper` applied to `spatial_gram`.
Reasoning: The analytic quadrature pads are astronomically small at orders 64 and 80 for radius 12, so the real numerical error in `w^T G_Q w`, `G_Q w`, and the power iterates is dominated by FINUFFT's relative tolerance of `1e-12` on inputs of magnitude near `||ell||^2` (about 37 in the fixed-weight case). That is roughly twenty times the 256-epsilon guard. In the previous case the residual squared is a `1e-6` relative cancellation, so a `1e-12` relative operator error is a `1e-6` relative error in the squared residual; harmless in size, but the protocol's phrase "ordinary-floating-point cancellation guard" should not be read as covering the operator. The same applies to `d` in the dual and to ARPACK/power iterations on a slightly nonsymmetric numerical operator. This is a disclosed limitation, not a validity error under the stated real-arithmetic scope.
Fix: At the final weights only, recompute `gram.matvec(w)` at `eps=1e-14` or by dense direct summation on a random node subset, and record `|delta(w^T G_Q w)|` and `||delta(G_Q w)||` as empirical pads added to the guard and to `d`. Label the guard as roundoff-only in the protocol.

**W2. Medium. The conic check is independent in program structure only, and its geometry cannot exercise multi-particle packing.**
Location: `tests/test_uq_cubic_optimization.py::independent_conic`, `fixture`.
Reasoning: The CVXPY model consumes `obj.op.matvec`, `obj.op.pair_moments`, `obj.coefficients`, `obj.kernel_error`, `obj.eta`, `obj.a`, `obj.target_norm2`, and `obj.remainder.blocks/multipliers/residual` directly. It independently assembles only the continuous sinc Gram and the cone structure. So agreement to `9.6e-9` shows the gradient path, the density majorant, and the dual bracket are consistent with the convex program; it does not test the field operator, the pads, or the remainder formula. The README says this, but the theory note's sentence "establish implementation agreement at the tested geometries" should be read that narrowly. Because the fixture has one particle, the degree-major versus particle-major column maps in `_to_particle_columns`, `_from_particle_columns`, and the real/imag `pack` layout are identical to the trivial ordering, so a transposition bug there would pass the bracket. The gradient tests in `test_uq_cubic_design.py` use two particles, which covers `moment_weight_gradient` and `weight_gradient`, but not `mass_terms`, `SobolevRemainderPenalty.value_gradient` inside the full objective, or the dual assembly across particles.
Fix: Add a two-particle, two-frequency conic bracket (8 coordinates, still cheap). Add to `test_full_objective_gradient_and_norm_supports` an assertion that `support @ other <= norm_sum` also holds when `evaluate` is given random unit spatial modes instead of exact singular vectors, which is the proposition actually relied on for partial Ritz returns.

**W3. Low. The recorded zero-weight regression does not characterize the failed conic solve.**

**W4. Low. Best-candidate selection uses an underestimating guide value.**
Location: `optimize_cubic_weights.evaluate`, `if value < best['value']`.
Reasoning: The retained candidate is the minimizer of the smoothed Ritz surrogate, whose spectral part is a lower estimate when modes are approximate or partial. Two evaluations can therefore be ordered differently by the guide than by their certified uppers, and only the selected one is certified. This affects tightness of the reported interval, not validity, because selection depends only on design quantities. The smoothing bound `B L smoothing log(modes)` only bounds the upward excess from entropy, not the downward error from inexact modes, so `selected_approximate_objective` should not be compared with `sum_objective_upper` beyond that caveat.
Fix: Report, at the selected weights, the certificate's `rayleigh_lower` against the recorded `ritz_singular_values` squared to show the underestimation magnitude. No extra probes are needed.

**W5. Low. Structural sources of gap, stated so the gap is not misread as pure optimization error.**

Reasoning: At a stationary point of the majorized, smoothed guide, the gradient contains `B eta w/H` and an entropy-weighted support, whereas `d` uses `a - G_Q w` without `eta w` and the guarded `Hbar`. Hence `c < 1` by roughly `f eta ||w||/z` plus the smoothing and guard effects, and the lower bound cannot reach the majorant's minimum. With `eta` at order-80 size this is negligible, but the theory note's statement "need not be tight for the majorant" is the correct reading, and the reported relative gap bounds suboptimality for the unsmoothed continuous surrogate only.
Fix: None required; mention these three contributions next to `gap_scope`.
