# Pose-estimation calibration before the end-to-end study

1 October 2026 UTC; motivated by review 2 R9/R11. Commit this protocol and its implementation before any calibration outcome. No confidence-interval coverage will be computed in this stage. Geometry and maps were previously inspected; this is simulation development, not a new experimental cohort.

Use 128 independent simulated datasets for each of EMPIAR 10028, 10049 and 10076 acquisition geometries: 128 particles, radius 5, geometry seed 609311. Truth is each already locked pilot-registered unit-L2 64-cell reference map. The 10076 map is a single Class A simulation generator, not heterogeneous experimental truth. Retain the original prescribed radius-five pilot-SNR noise. Draw independent N(0,1) real/imaginary noise in the whitened observation model. All dataset noise and initialization seeds derive from SeedSequence([261001, dataset, replicate]).

For each simulated dataset refine poses against both (a) the fixed true generator (oracle-template control) and (b) the existing independent pilot's unit-L2 24-cell density (template-mismatch control). These are local refinements, not unknown-pose global reconstruction. Initialization has independent Gaussian rotations with total RMS 2 degrees and translations with total RMS 0.5 Angstrom around the true pose. Do not select favorable initializations or retry fits.

Use ten iterations of damped Gauss–Newton, damping .01, per-step rotation limit 2 degrees and shift limit 1 Angstrom, three candidate line-search factors (1,.5,.25) plus unchanged iterate. Bound total movement from the INITIALIZATION by 20 degrees and 10 Angstrom. The optimizer must not use truth in these bounds. Re-evaluate the true nonlinear constant-cell projections for each candidate; derivatives are analytic Fourier moments. Record all objective histories, boundary counts and pose errors, including worse or boundary-limited outcomes.

Preserve per-dataset/replicate/template JSON and paired NPZ arrays, exact inputs, source snapshots, execution time and memory. Do not overwrite or drop failed outcomes. Summarize angular/translation RMS, maxima, coordinate bias and error-distribution shape before declaring the subsequent interval study. Calibration extrema/quantiles will be labelled empirical, not certified deterministic radii or experimental confidence sets. A statistically justified tolerance construction requires a separately stated exchangeability argument and budget.

The follow-up study must freeze its interval rules and any selected radii before at least 200 additional independent replicate datasets per geometry. It will recompute weights after local refinement and compare same-image vs independent inference-noise controls. This calibration protocol does not yet freeze the follow-up interval study or imply that its end-to-end coverage has been demonstrated.
