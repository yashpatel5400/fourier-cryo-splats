# Targeted primary-source checks following review 3

1 October 2026 UTC. These are an explicit addendum to the existing 131-candidate
ledger, not 131 full readings or a silently enlarged historical count.

- **Kanagawa, Hennig, Sejdinovic and Sriperumbudur (2025),**
  [Gaussian Processes and Reproducing Kernels](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.17366v1).
  Read Sections 6.5–6.6 and the introductory account of the earlier 2018 report.
  Posterior means equal kernel-ridge estimates under the matching regularizer.
  The regularized-kernel worst-error interpretation includes observation noise;
  it must not be confused with a density-only class. The paper now credits the
  established connection instead of implying a new Bayesian/frequentist divide.
  This is a targeted reading, not a full review of the monograph.
- **Fiedler, Scherer and Trimpe (2021),**
  [corrected preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.02796v2).
  Read Theorem 1, Proposition 2, the correction note and the relevant proof
  decomposition. The guarantees require a norm bound and specified noise/design
  conditions. The v2 correction changes constants involving the nominal noise
  variance; the original conference statement should not be copied unchecked.
  These results do not automatically cover same-image estimated orientations.
- **Beckers, Palmer and Sachse (2020),**
  [Confidence maps](https://journals.iucr.org/d/issues/2020/04/00/rr5194/rr5194.pdf).
  Verified title, authors, year, DOI and the introductory distinction between
  background significance and structural correctness. This is the paper read
  in `PRIMARY-ANNOTATIONS.md`. It is distinct from Beckers, Jakobi and Sachse
  (2019), which remains cited as the preceding FDR method. Both now have their
  own bibliography entries; the reading record is no longer attributed only
  to the 2019 source.

Access was through primary arXiv HTML and the publisher PDF. A PMC browser
check blocked direct access there; the publisher PDF was accessible. Additional
reviewer-suggested incidental-parameter/MRA references remain candidates until
their primary claims are checked; a model review's recollection is not a source.
