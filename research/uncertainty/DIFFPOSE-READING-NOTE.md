# Pose spread versus confidence radii

Targeted primary reading, 30 September 2026. [DiffPose](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.22082.85441)
is an October 2025 author-uploaded preprint using a fixed molecular template
and neural projection decoder. Its uncertainty score measures angular spread
among five nearby converged trajectories; simulated errors increase with spread.
Translation search is left for future work.

Our assessment: this reliability score does not establish specified-coverage
pose balls on experimental particles. Shared incorrect minima and template bias
need separate calibration before such a score could supply our nuisance radii.
